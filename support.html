Until

A timer you can see from across the room.

How it works

Set a time
Drag up anywhere. The higher you drag, the longer the time; a full screen is 60 minutes.
Start
Let go and tap Start. The color goes down as the time runs out.
Pause, resume or stop
Tap the time to pause. Tap Resume to go on, or Stop to clear.
Change a running timer
Hold until the phone buzzes, then drag.
15 minutes full screen
On the empty screen, swipe left and choose 15 instead of 60.

Questions

The timer didn't ring when the phone was locked
Until needs permission for alarms. Open Settings, tap Until and turn on Alarms and Timers.
Can I keep the screen on?
The screen stays on by itself while Until is open and a timer runs.
I closed the app and the alarm still rang
Once you leave the app, the alarm is handed to iOS so it rings even if the app is closed. Stop it from the Lock Screen or the Dynamic Island.
